    OUR STORY

    NEED

    Women in their 20's seek control and structure in their life. Without this, their mental health suffers. One in four women take mental health medication compared to 15% of men. Women have 2x higher anxiety levels than men. Millennials are the most anxious generation. The average age at onset of depression is mid-20s.

    MISSION

    We are a global team of women in our 20's building Reva. Through Reva, we are empowering women to achieve their potential through professional development, personal growth, and wellness.

    LEGACY

    Reva Myers (1938 - 2010) was born in Urbana, Illinois. She was kind, gritty, and selfless. Being loyal was her trademark. Our values as a team, app, and community are inspired by her.
